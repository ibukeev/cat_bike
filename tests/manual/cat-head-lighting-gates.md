# Cat Head Lighting Manual Gate Checklist

Use this checklist with the formal 330 mm cat-head lighting development plan.
Do not mark a gate complete without recording measurements, photographs, parts,
date, and test operator.

## Procurement and Inventory

- [x] Order 2.0 mm bare side-glow PMMA fiber.
  - Quantity: 9 m
  - Ordered: 2026-07-14
  - Expected: 2026-07-16
- [ ] Record fiber manufacturer, seller, product link, and lot/part identifier.
- [ ] Identify existing LED voltage, protocol, package, density/spacing, and
      available pixel count.
- [ ] Confirm a current-limited 5 V bench supply is available.
- [ ] Set aside temporary 5 V, ground, and data leads/connectors.
- [ ] Obtain or set aside black heat-shrink/opaque coupling tube, a fresh razor
      or POF cutter, and fine abrasive.
- [ ] Confirm whether existing LEDs can provide seven individually addressable
      pixels in a carrier-compatible arrangement.

## First Fiber Coupon

- [ ] Cut one sacrificial 350 mm fiber length; do not cut final blanks yet.
- [ ] Photograph and record the unprepared input face.
- [ ] Prepare a square input face and record the tool and abrasive sequence.
- [ ] Couple one existing 5 V RGB pixel to the fiber with opaque tubing.
- [ ] Record LED type, coupling gap, voltage, commanded brightness, and current.
- [ ] Photograph root, middle, and far end with fixed exposure at 20%, 35%, and
      60% brightness.
- [ ] Confirm full-length visibility from 3 m in darkness at 35%.
- [ ] Confirm far-end brightness is at least 40% of root brightness.
- [ ] Test the rounded/soft-capped external tip for sharpness and retention.

## Seven-Whisker Cheek Coupon

- [ ] Build one opaque seven-cell carrier using the measured LED geometry.
- [ ] Couple seven separate pixels to seven separate fibers.
- [ ] Confirm one strand can be replaced without disturbing the other six.
- [ ] Calibrate and record pixel-to-pixel brightness variation.
- [ ] Apply a 5 N pull for 10 seconds to every fiber and cap.
- [ ] Complete 200 controlled port flex cycles without slip, crack, or sharp edge.
- [ ] Confirm at least a 12 mm bend radius everywhere inside the coupon.

## Eye Coupon

- [ ] Build a four-pixel carrier and black light cup.
- [ ] Compare 1.0 and 1.5 mm frosted PETG.
- [ ] Compare 20, 25, and 30 mm LED setbacks.
- [ ] Confirm no individual LED is visible from 1 m.
- [ ] Record minimum/maximum surface brightness and left/right build process.

## Facet Coupon

- [ ] Build one representative full-size two-pixel glow panel.
- [ ] Add opaque perimeter baffles and a reflective internal cavity.
- [ ] Confirm no raw LED is visible from 1 m at riding brightness.
- [ ] Confirm adjacent opaque material remains visibly dark.

## Complete Head

- [ ] Verify 52 physical pixels and reserved pixels H07/H51 always black.
- [ ] Measure full-white current; pass at no more than 3.2 A.
- [ ] Measure farthest-module voltage; pass at 4.5 V or greater.
- [ ] Run 30 minutes at full load and measure M12 temperature rise.
- [ ] Run one hour in show mode and record internal temperature.
- [ ] Complete data-integrity, dust, gentle splash, vibration, service-access,
      steering, headlight, rider-clearance, and night-photo tests.
- [ ] Confirm whisker span is no more than 50 mm wider than the handlebars.
