Use built-in `image_gen`. Begin with the corrected placement mockup as the only conversation image, then use each latest result as the sole input for the next option (`num_last_images_to_include: 1`). Preserve the real bicycle, geometry, background, and dusk treatment in every edit.

`Replace the existing LED routing with exactly three elements: one continuous cyan/teal lower-frame strip following the rear lower frame through the step-through belly curve; one continuous cyan/teal upper rear haunch strip beneath the seat/rack ending under the basket; and one short downward-facing basket-underlight. Remove vertical torso strips and accents. Keep generous dark negative space.`

Add 6–8 straight strip strokes: 4–5 forming two incomplete angular chevrons/open facets in the fixed rear-rack triangle, two short lower-frame strokes forming a shallow forward chevron, one basket-underlight, and one short magenta facet edge. `No LEDs on forks, wheels, tires, spokes, brake rotors, chain, crank, steering components, cables, or moving parts. No illuminated panels or cat head. No labels, arrows, text, or watermark.
