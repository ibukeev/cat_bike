# Hardware

Use this area for electrical and mechanical design files.

- `electrical/battery/` - battery pack specs, charging notes, fuses, switches, and power budget.
- `electrical/controller-pixelblaze/` - Pixelblaze wiring, pinout, configuration, and controller mounting.
- `electrical/leds/` - LED strip or pixel specs, counts, layout, and power injection points.
- `electrical/wiring/` - cable plans, connector pinouts, harness drawings, and routing notes.
- `mechanical/mounts/` - brackets, clips, ties, and bike attachment parts.
- `mechanical/enclosures/` - controller, battery, and weather protection designs.
