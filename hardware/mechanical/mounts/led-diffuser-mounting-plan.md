# LED Diffuser and Mounting Plan

## Purpose

Plan how LED strips, diffusers, and small light modules attach to the bike without making the build look unfinished or damaging the bike.

## Hard Rules

- No permanent glue directly on the painted bike frame.
- No adhesive-only final LED mounting.
- Every LED segment needs mechanical retention or a protected mounting channel.
- Every solder joint or connector needs strain relief.
- Mounting must be removable after Burning Man where practical.

## Paint-Safe Mounting Preference

| Method | Paint Risk | Best Use | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Rubber-lined clamps | Low | Brackets, channels, battery/electronics supports | Best for removable structural mounting. |
| Zip ties / reusable cable ties | Low | LED segments, wire retention, temporary tests | Use neatly, same spacing/color, trimmed flush. |
| Silicone bands / Velcro straps | Low | Temporary/removable light modules | Better for modules than bare strips. |
| Clip-on mounts with rubber padding | Low | Repeated LED segments, fork/rack accents | Good candidate for custom 3D printed mounts. |
| VHB on removable channel/bracket | Low-medium | Attaching LEDs to a removable carrier | Prefer adhesive on the carrier, not bike paint. |
| VHB directly on painted frame | Medium | Temporary or semi-permanent tests only | Test hidden spot; may leave residue or pull weak paint. |
| Silicone adhesive / E6000 / Shoe Goo on painted frame | High | Avoid on bike paint | Use only on removable parts if needed. |
| Epoxy / superglue | High | Avoid | Too permanent for this project. |

## Design Rule

Visible waterproof LED strip is allowed when it is treated as a deliberate segmented pixel graphic. Hidden glow or smooth light should use diffusion.

Direct visible strip looks better when:

- It is cut into clean, repeated segments.
- Segments align with frame/rack geometry.
- Wire exits are hidden or strain-relieved.
- Brightness is controlled.
- It is mechanically retained beyond adhesive.

Avoid:

- Long continuous outline tracing the whole bike.
- Randomly placed strip sections.
- Exposed messy pigtails.
- Adhesive-only strips on dusty/curved frame tubes.

## Zone Recommendations

| Zone | Preferred Mounting | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Lower frame segmented spine | Waterproof strip or diffuser segments on removable clips/channels; VHB on carrier plus zip ties/clips | Keep away from pedals, kickstand, chain, battery access, and foot strike. |
| Fork gill accents | Short protected pods, clips, or small channels | Avoid tire, brake rotor, brake cable, and fork movement. |
| Rear rack rib accents | Direct waterproof strip with neat zip ties, or aluminum/plastic channel | Rack is a good mechanical mounting surface. |
| Basket underside glow | Hidden waterproof strip under basket lip/rail with VHB plus clips/ties | Basket remains usable; avoid basket-face string lights. |
| Ground underglow | Hidden inboard strip/channel with mechanical backup | Must not be lowest point or exposed to curb/transport hits. |

## Wire Mounting Rules

- Secure wire every few inches along frame/rack where practical.
- Route wires away from wheels, chain, pedals, brake rotors, and steering pinch points.
- Use service loops only inside protected areas.
- Route data with ground.
- Do not bind brake/control cables tightly with LED wiring.
- Add strain relief before each LED segment and connector.

## Removal Guidance

- Prefer removable brackets, clips, clamps, straps, and zip ties over direct adhesive.
- If VHB touches bike paint, remove slowly with heat and adhesive remover rather than scraping.
- Expect any direct adhesive on paint to be semi-permanent and test in a hidden spot first.
