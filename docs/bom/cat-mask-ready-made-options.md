## Decision Criteria

- **Best buy-first candidate:** Fusion Layer 3D Hexa Cat Mask. It already has a geometric cat shape and glow-accent concept close to the project direction.
- **Best showpiece ready-made option:** MirrorLEDCostumes Low Poly Mirror Plastic Cat Mask, if the budget and shipping risk are acceptable.
- **Best buy-the-LED-effect option:** MirrorLEDCostumes smart LED mirror cat mask, if its controller, runtime, and power setup work with the bike.
- **Best modifiable rigid base:** 3D Planet Props White Cat Mask, especially if the raw or primed option is available.
- **Best cheap shape prototype:** Papercraft PDF options, but only for mockups unless reinforced and sealed.
- **Best stronger DIY template:** PaleoPanthera big-cat low-poly pattern in cardboard or EVA foam.
- **Lowest-cost wearable fallback:** Etsy leather cat masks, but treat them as costume pieces rather than electronics mounting surfaces.
- **Avoid unless intentionally building around it:** paper mache or high-detail art masks, because dust, weather, and repairability may become project risks.

## Questions Before Ordering

- Does the mask fit the rider comfortably with goggles, dust mask, and helmet/hat setup?
- Is there enough rigid surface for LED diffusion, wiring strain relief, and serviceable attachment points?
- Can it survive playa dust, bumps, UV, and transport without cracking or delaminating?
- Are there existing channels, whiskers, holes, or edge features that can hide LED wires?
- What is the real shipping lead time and return policy?
