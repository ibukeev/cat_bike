## First-Pass Layout Annotations

### Front Area

- The existing front headlight sits low and forward; do not block it unless it is intentionally replaced.
- The handlebar and head tube area are crowded with brake cables and controls, so the cat head should either mount to a dedicated bracket or use a carefully placed handlebar/stem clamp.
- Front fork lighting can run down the fork legs, but must stay clear of the tire, brake rotor, and cable motion.
- The cat head should be removable and should have a service connector near the head tube or stem.

### Side Frame

- The lower frame tube is the strongest candidate for Phase 1 underglow because it is independent of future sculptural panels.
- The large battery occupies the central frame area; do not block battery removal, key access, or charging access.
- The step-through frame creates a good visible side silhouette, but panel work should remain removable.
- Pedal, chain, and kickstand clearance should be treated as no-go zones for wiring and panels.

### Rear Rack and Basket

- The rear rack and basket provide strong mounting structure for Phase 1 rear lighting.
- Basket perimeter lighting is useful for rear/side visibility and does not depend on body panels.
- Tail mounting should use the rack/basket structure, with a quick disconnect and a secondary safety tether.
- Tail design must preserve cargo use unless cargo is intentionally sacrificed for the event build.

### Body Panels

- Body panels should be treated as Phase 3 because LED placement may depend on the inside face of the panel.
- Phase 1 should reserve power/data connectors for future panel lighting instead of installing those LEDs early.
- Candidate panel zones are the side frame opening, rear rack side, and front lower frame/fork transition.

### Wheels

- Wheel effects remain Phase 4. They are visually strong but add durability, power, and maintenance complexity.
- Any wheel lighting must avoid spokes, brake rotors, tires, valve stems, and wheel removal/service.

## Phase 1 Reference Review

Current visual reference: [phase1-led-reference-v3-current.png](../../assets/photos/Concepts/Bio-Luminescent%20Abyssinian/Phase_1_LED_References/phase1-led-reference-v3-current.png)

What to keep:

- Restrained lower frame lighting with a segmented addressable feel.
- Short fork accents instead of full fork outlines.
- Rear rack support accents.
- Subtle hidden basket underside glow.
- Soft ground underglow.
- Cyan/aqua base palette with small violet/magenta pulse accents.
- Unlit flag post.

What to reject from earlier iterations:

- Wheel rings, tire lights, spoke lights, and wheel effects.
- Handlebar, grip, brake lever, or control-area lighting.
- Dense basket-face lighting or woven basket string lights.
- Random magenta dots not tied to a real endpoint or junction.
- Any literal geometry that cannot be mounted cleanly with removable, protected LED channels.

## Phase 1 Candidate LED Runs

| Zone | Rough Length Estimate | Why It Belongs in Phase 1 |
|---|---:|---|
| Lower frame underglow | 30-45 in per side | Independent of panels, high visibility, protected if mounted inboard. |
| Fork legs | 18-24 in per fork leg | Improves front visibility; must be mechanically protected. |
| Rear rack perimeter | 40-70 in | Strong mounting structure and rear visibility. |
| Basket front/rear faces | 30-60 in total | High visibility, easy to mount, removable with basket work. |

## Reserved Interfaces

| Future Zone | Reserve Location | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Cat head | Head tube/stem area | Needs power/data quick disconnect and strain relief. |

| Wheels | Near fork/rear triangle only if pursued | Defer until fixed lighting is stable. |
