# Bio-Luminescent Abyssinian Concept

## Direction

The current visual direction is a Bio-Luminescent Abyssinian cat bike: a warm gold RadRunner base with a faceted copper or rose-gold cat identity, cyan/aqua/magenta glow, fiber-optic whiskers, and an illuminated tail.

## Keeper Elements

- Gold/yellow bike frame remains visible as the base structure.
- Faceted copper or rose-gold cat head mounted at the front.
- Bright cyan/aqua eyes.
- Cyan, aqua, magenta, and soft violet LED palette.
- Fiber-optic whiskers as a signature front detail.
- Illuminated tail on or near the rear rack.
- Body glow along frame, basket, rack, and optional translucent panels.
- Optional wheel effects for a later showpiece phase.

## Build Interpretation

The concept should be translated into serviceable, removable layers rather than a single large shell. The priority is a durable rideable bike first, then sculpture and show effects.

## Phases

### Playa-Ready Minimum

- Frame, fork, rear rack, and basket lighting.
- Controller, battery, fuse, switch, and enclosure.
- Basic bioluminescent Pixelblaze patterns.
- Strain relief and dust-resistant wiring.

### Cat Identity Layer

- Cat head or mask.
- Illuminated eyes.
- Fiber-optic whiskers.
- Illuminated tail.
- Copper or rose-gold faceted finish.

### Showpiece Upgrades

- Diffuser panels or translucent body ribs.
- Wheel lighting.
- More advanced segmented patterns.
- Parked/show mode.

## Constraints

- Do not block steering, braking, headlight output, rider sightline, battery removal, or cargo use.
- Keep sculptural parts removable for transport and repair.
- Avoid fragile, low-clearance, or hard-to-service attachments.
- Prioritize nighttime readability from front, side, and rear.

## Open Questions

- Should the cat head attach to the handlebar, head tube, front rack area, or a separate bracket?
- Should the whiskers be passive side-glow fiber, active LED filament, or fiber driven by hidden LEDs?
- Should the tail be rigid, flexible, or semi-flexible?
- Are wheel effects worth including in the first build?
